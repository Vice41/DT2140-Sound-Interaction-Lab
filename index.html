    • Interaction 1: Fictional Laser Gun
    It transforms your device into a fictional laser gun. To fire the laser, tilt the phone on the x-axis. The device will emit a laser sound effect with each tilt gesture. To reload and prepare for the next shot, pull the phone backwards on the y-axis.

    Interaction 2: Roulette / Wheel of Fortune
    This interaction turns your phone into a virtual roulette wheel. When placed flat on a surface, spinning the phone initiates the roulette effect. As the device rotates around the z-axis, rhythmic clicking sounds are produced, mimicking the ticks of a spinning wheel. The click frequency follows the rotation speed, slowing down as the phone stops.

    Interaction 3: Thor's hammer
    This interaction transforms the phone into a lightning-charging tool. Lifting the phone upward creates a charging effect. Once the phone is “charged,” shake it to trigger the thunder rumbling sound.
